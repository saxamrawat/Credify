CAPACITY DECISION

Review queue

How your review capacity shaped the ranked customer list.

How selection works

A consistent selection rule determines who enters the review list.

↕
  1. Rank by predicted risk.Customers are ordered from highest to lowest predicted default probability. Equal scores receive the same rank, so later ranks account for all customers ahead.
  2. Keep equal scores together.Customers with the same risk score enter the review list together when the group fits within your capacity.
  3. Stop at a boundary tie.If the next tied group would exceed the remaining target, that whole group and all lower-scoring groups are left out.
To change capacity: edit Review capacity above and click Run analysis. This queue shows the result from the last completed run.
